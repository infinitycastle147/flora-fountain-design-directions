## Content integrity notes for prototype team

1. **Keep brand facts and copy source-linked.** Avoid replacing Flora Fountain with a generic agency, changing 150+/50+/1BN+ proof points, or inventing client names and outcomes.
2. **The source has inconsistencies:** homepage says 10+ years, industry overview says 11 years; homepage proof sometimes says 150+ projects and elsewhere 150+ brands; its differentiator block duplicates Google expertise; the FAQ repeats one question. For initial prototypes, prefer the homepage’s 10+, 150+ projects/brands as context, 50+, 65M+, 20M+, 1BN+, and flag final wording for client approval.
3. **Service nomenclature varies by context:** “Website Design” on the home card, “Website Development” in the menu, “Website Design and Development” in the footer; “Content Creation” home card leads to Video Creation. Preserve this mapping or request copy approval before changing labels.
4. **Current client logos, portraits, project imagery, and case results need asset rights/approval** for the final production build. The prototype may use verified live source imagery to demonstrate direction, with attribution in the design review.
5. **Blogs, testimonial ordering, and case-study ordering may change** automatically. This inventory records what was visible on 15 October 2026.
