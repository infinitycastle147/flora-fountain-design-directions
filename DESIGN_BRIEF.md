# Flora Fountain — prototype design brief

**Decision to present:** Three responsive visual directions for the same Flora Fountain content. The client should be able to compare the theme they supplied against a careful evolution of it and one distinct recommendation. These are design prototypes for review; dynamic content and CMS work can follow the selected direction.

**Reference of record:** [Inbounderz theme audit](THEME_REFERENCE.md) and [Flora Fountain content audit](CONTENT_REFERENCE.md), including their local screenshots. Inbounderz supplies visual language only. Flora Fountain supplies the brand, words, services, proof, people, images, links, and contact facts.

## Shared product requirements

- Design first for a prospective client deciding whether Flora Fountain understands their business and can deliver growth. Within the first viewport, show the agency positioning, the `150+ brands in Ahmedabad` trust statement, and a clear contact action.
- Give all three options the same information architecture and core copy so feedback is about design, not different messages. Use the Flora Fountain logo and verified brand imagery. Never show Inbounderz people, work, awards, statistics, or logos as Flora Fountain's.
- Build a navigable, responsive static frontend with working menu, section anchors, case links, FAQ controls, and contact route/action. Forms can be demonstrative until a backend is agreed, but must clearly say when submission is not active.
- Deliver polished desktop and mobile views. Preserve readable type, keyboard access, visible focus, sensible contrast, reduced-motion behavior, and zero horizontal page overflow.

## Direction A — Faithful reference

**Role:** Give the client a close interpretation of the theme they selected.

Use Inbounderz's nearly black opening (`#090909`), Manrope and Georama pairing, transparent overlay header, mixed-weight centered headline, huge low-contrast background type/number, grayscale Flora Fountain team montage, slim yellow (`#ffca2a`) CTA treatment, service marquee, and full-width typographic service rows. Continue with alternating dark and white editorial bands, yellow proof numerals, oversized agency story title, charcoal case gallery, testimonials, FAQ, and a light footer. Follow the measured spacing and section proportions in the theme audit where they suit Flora's nine services. Use Flora's “we love problems” motif as a brand-specific element; do not copy Inbounderz's “360” claim as a fact.

**Distinctive first impression:** The theme's cinematic agency stage, unmistakably filled with Flora Fountain content.

## Direction B — Restrained evolution

**Role:** Preserve the client's theme preference while making its long page easier to scan.

Retain the dark/white/yellow palette, Manrope-led typography, large editorial headlines, grayscale imagery, marquee accent, and horizontal service language. Introduce a more structured hero with a left-aligned message and right-side branded artwork or team image, slightly shorter sections, tighter content hierarchy, and alternating service rows grouped in a clear nine-item grid/list. Use the same yellow mainly for CTAs and key figures. Cases should be more legible at a glance, with title, client, and verified outcome visible without hover. Navigation and contact should stay consistently available.

**Distinctive first impression:** Familiar reference DNA with more immediate clarity and faster access to proof.

## Direction C — Recommended original

**Role:** Propose a Flora Fountain identity that can scale beyond a borrowed theme.

Build around Flora's own “we love problems” idea: a warm, editorial strategy studio with an expressive typographic hero, intentional asymmetric composition, Flora brand imagery, and a repeatable visual device drawn from questions, problem statements, and growth paths. Use deep ink and warm paper as the base, with a restrained citrus accent; keep proof and CTAs prominent. Present nine services as an organized capability system, not nine identical cards. Place selected client work and measurable outcomes early, then the agency story, process, sector expertise, testimonials, and contact. Motion should support the story through controlled reveal and hover behavior rather than dominate it.

**Distinctive first impression:** A confident Flora Fountain brand system, visibly independent from Inbounderz while carrying exactly the same content.

## Required homepage content and order

Order may shift modestly to suit each layout, but each prototype must include all these content groups:

1. **Header and hero:** Flora Fountain logo; primary links for Services, Industries, Portfolio or Client Success Stories, About Us, Blogs, and Contact Us; original positioning and `150+ brands` statement; `LET'S TALK` CTA.
2. **Trust and story:** `Trusted Professionals at Your Service`; `10+ Years`, `150+ Projects`, `50+ Experts`; verified client logos where practical; `The Creative Universe of Flora Fountain`; founded in 2016 and the source introduction; About CTA.
3. **Nine services:** exact homepage labels, lead lines, outcome lines, and destination mapping in the content reference; `GET YOUR GROWTH STRATEGY` CTA. A compact presentation may reveal the second line on expansion, but it must remain accessible.
4. **Why Flora and process:** current differentiator proof, `1BN+ impressions`, `20M+ organic traffic`; four steps `Strategise`, `Create`, `Execute`, `Measure`; results `65M+ Likes`, `20M+ Organic Traffic`, `1BN+ Impressions`.
5. **Sectors and work:** four homepage industries (Hospitality, Real Estate, FMCG, Jewellery); at least the three verified homepage case studies (CarzSpa, Ajay's Cafe, Vimal Oil) and their actual titles/results.
6. **Human proof and editorial:** at least one full named source testimonial, the `Hear From Our Clients` section, the three currently listed article titles or links, and FAQ entries from the reference.
7. **Conversion and footer:** `You've scrolled this far. Clearly, we should talk.`; business phone and email; contact form fields; career email; verified India and USA addresses in footer or Contact page.

**Supporting routes for a coherent prototype:** About (verified hero, timeline, founders and philosophy), Services overview (the nine mapped offers), Client Success Stories or Portfolio index (only verified cases), and Contact (verified statement, details and form). Other menu items may link to the live source until their content is audited and built. Do not fabricate content for unaudited portfolio details or service pages.

## Content preservation rules

- Use the exact homepage copy bank in `CONTENT_REFERENCE.md` for hero, service lead/outcome lines, differentiators, process, FAQs, and selected testimonials. Visual layout may split text across lines or disclosure panels; do not rewrite its claims for aesthetic fit.
- Keep Flora Fountain's names, numerical claims, case outcomes, founder roles, phone numbers, emails, and office addresses as recorded. Keep `150+ brands` in the hero and `150+ Projects` in the trust counter; do not imply they are the same metric.
- The source contains a repeated FAQ question, two near-duplicate Google credentials, and a 10/11-year discrepancy. The prototype can display a single instance of repeated content for usability, but must not invent replacement copy. Flag these three editorial questions for client approval before launch.
- Use only verified client logos, case images, portraits, and testimonials from the source inventory; mark unavailable assets for replacement. Final publication requires confirmation that the client can reuse them.
- Do not add invented success numbers, quotes, customers, awards, certifications, industries, or SEO claims. Keep current article links as provisional because the blog may change.

## Client review criteria

Ask the client to choose a direction based on: (1) closeness to the supplied Inbounderz theme, (2) whether the hero feels like Flora Fountain, (3) clarity of services and proof in the first two scrolls, (4) quality of mobile experience, and (5) confidence that the visual system can extend to About, Services, cases, and Contact. Record copy corrections and asset approvals separately from the visual choice. The recommendation is **Direction C** if the client wants a durable Flora identity; **Direction A** is the fidelity benchmark and **Direction B** the safer evolution.
