## Editorial decisions needed before a production release

1. The homepage says **10+ Years**; the Industries overview says **11 years**. Use the homepage wording in prototypes and ask the client to approve final tenure.
2. The homepage calls out **150+ Projects** in one counter and **150+ brands** elsewhere. Keep each metric in its original context; do not treat them as interchangeable.
3. **Certified Google Partner** and **Certified Google Expertise** are near-duplicate differentiators. They are both part of the captured copy; a shortened display may use the former alone, pending approval.
4. The last FAQ repeats question three. Preserve the duplication in this record; a compact UI may show five unique questions.
5. Service names vary: **Website Design** on home, **Website Development** in navigation, **Website Design and Development** in footer. **Content Creation** on home links to Video Creation. Use context-specific labels until the client approves a consistent naming system.
6. The source has client logos, quotes, case images, and claim figures. Verify rights and currency before publication. Prototype images are reference assets, not an assertion of production rights.
